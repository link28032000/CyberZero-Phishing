================================================================
CYBERZERO — GAMEPLAY & SCORING REFERENCE
================================================================

OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------
You play a newly hired Cyber Detective inside a simulated Windows
desktop with exactly two apps: Gmail and Browser. Your mission is
to investigate 5 emails, find the real evidence of phishing (or
confirm an email is safe), flag that evidence, and report each
email as PHISHING or LEGITIMATE. The Capybara Detective reviews
every submission and explains what you got right, wrong, or
missed.

This is not a guessing quiz — you have to find and flag the
specific suspicious parts of an email (or correctly recognize
that there's nothing suspicious to flag) before you report it.


GAMEPLAY FLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Welcome & briefing — no clock yet. Capybara/Detective Zero
   introduces what phishing looks like: fake senders, false
   urgency, mismatched links.
2. Live demo — Detective Zero personally investigates one
   phishing email and one legitimate email, placing flags and
   narrating exactly how many points each action is worth. The
   demo also shows how to undo a flag placed by mistake: click
   the same flagged spot again and it toggles off, no penalty,
   as long as it's removed before you report.
3. "Your turn, Detective." Gmail opens with 5 unread emails.
4. For each email:
     - Open it and read the sender, subject, and body carefully.
     - Turn on FLAG EVIDENCE mode, then click anything you think
       is suspicious (sender address, an urgent line, a link).
       Each click drops a flag into the Evidence panel.
     - Click a flagged item again to remove it if you change
       your mind — this works at any point before you report.
     - Open the Browser app if a link looks suspicious, to see
       exactly where it goes. Switching between Gmail and
       Browser never resets your progress.
     - Click "Report Email" and choose REPORT AS PHISHING or
       MARK AS LEGITIMATE.
     - Capybara reviews your submission immediately: confirms
       your verdict, praises correct flags, explains incorrect
       flags, points out anything missed, and shows a full
       line-by-line score breakdown.
     - Click "Next Email" and repeat.
5. Emails 1–3 are completely untimed — investigate at your own
   pace.
6. Right after Email 3's review, Capybara warns that the final
   two emails are timed. Each of Emails 4 and 5 gets its own
   2-minute clock that only starts once you confirm you're
   ready.
7. If a clock hits zero before you submit, Capybara auto-submits
   whatever you currently have:
     - Decision is inferred as Phishing if you've placed 1+
       flags, or Legitimate if you've placed none.
     - Whatever flags are on the board are scored exactly as if
       you'd submitted them manually.
     - Scoring follows the normal rules, so an incomplete or
       wrong auto-submit still earns partial credit — never a
       hard zero.
     - The review screen shows a "TIME EXPIRED" banner on any
       auto-submitted email.
8. After Email 5, you reach the Mission Complete screen with
   full stats, final score, and Detective Rank.


THE FIVE-EMAIL MISSION
----------------------------------------------------------------
  #   Type          Evidence to Find
  1   Phishing      Fake sender + false urgency + suspicious link
  2   Legitimate    Normal company email — don't flag it
  3   Phishing      Fake sender + password threat + suspicious link
  4   Legitimate    Normal internal email — don't flag it
  5   Phishing      Fake sender + reward scam + suspicious link


FLAG-BASED INVESTIGATION
----------------------------------------------------------------
Turn on Flag Mode, then click directly on suspicious evidence.

What to flag:
  fake_sender             e.g. security@bpi-secure-login.xyz
  false_urgency           e.g. "account suspended in 30 minutes"
  suspicious_link         button text vs. real destination don't match
  suspicious_attachment   unexpected .zip / .exe attached
  credential_request      e.g. "enter your PIN to confirm"
  suspicious_wording      unusual phrasing or grammatical errors

You can remove a flag if it was placed incorrectly — click the
same flagged spot again and it toggles off. This works any time
before you submit the report; nothing is locked in until then.


ONE SUBMISSION PER EMAIL — BUT INVESTIGATE FREELY
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Submitting a report is final. Once you click "Report Email"
  (or the clock auto-submits for you), that email is locked —
  no resubmitting or changing the verdict.
- You can still reopen and re-read any email at any time:
    - Reopening your current, still-in-progress email resumes
      exactly where you left off, flags included.
    - Reopening a past, already-reported email shows it in
      read-only review mode with your submitted flags
      highlighted and a banner confirming the case is closed.
- Closing or minimizing Gmail or Browser never loses progress.


SCORING SYSTEM, EXPLAINED
----------------------------------------------------------------
Every email you report earns (or loses) points from two
independent sources, added together:
  1. Decision points — for correctly calling the email
     Phishing or Legitimate.
  2. Evidence points — for each flag you placed, scored
     individually as correct or incorrect.

1) DECISION POINTS
  Situation                                                Points
  Phishing email, you say Phishing, backed by 1+ correct
    flags                                                   +100
  Phishing email, you say Phishing, but zero correct
    flags (a lucky guess)                                     +0
  Legitimate email, you say Legitimate                        +50
  Any email, wrong verdict                                   -50

  Why phishing calls are worth more: a correct "Phishing"
  verdict only pays out if backed by real evidence — you have
  to have actually found and flagged something true, not just
  guessed right. A "Legitimate" verdict doesn't require finding
  anything (there's nothing to flag), so it's worth half as
  much.

2) EVIDENCE (FLAG) POINTS
  Every flag you place is checked against that email's real
  evidence list.
  Flag result                                              Points
  Correct flag (matches real evidence on a phishing email)   +25
  Incorrect flag (wrong spot on a phishing email, or ANY
    flag on a legitimate email)                              -10

  Real evidence you never flagged isn't penalized directly —
  it shows up as "Missed Evidence" in Capybara's review, so
  you learn what to look for next time.

3) THE CONTRADICTION RULE: FLAGS + "LEGITIMATE" = 0 POINTS
  If you place one or more flags and then submit the email as
  Legitimate, the whole report scores a flat 0 — no decision
  points, no flag points, no penalties either way. Flagging
  something as suspicious and then clearing the email doesn't
  reflect a real verdict, so Capybara throws the report out
  rather than scoring it. The Report dialog always warns you
  about this up front whenever you have flags placed.

WORKED EXAMPLE — ONE PHISHING EMAIL
  You investigate "BPI Security" (a phishing email with 3 real
  pieces of evidence: fake sender, false urgency, suspicious
  link), correctly flag all 3, and report it as Phishing:

  Line item                                                Points
  Correct flag — Fake Sender                                 +25
  Correct flag — False Urgency                                +25
  Correct flag — Suspicious Link                              +25
  Decision — Phishing correctly identified, backed by
    evidence                                                 +100
  TOTAL                                                      +175

  This is exactly what the Capybara review screen shows,
  line by line, for every single email.

RUNNING TOTAL ACROSS THE WHOLE MISSION
  The mission has 5 emails: 3 phishing (each worth up to +175:
  100 decision + 75 for its 3 flags) and 2 legitimate (each
  worth up to +50, no flags needed). A perfect run:

  Email   Type          Best possible score
  1       Phishing      +175
  2       Legitimate    +50
  3       Phishing      +175
  4       Legitimate    +50
  5       Phishing      +175
  Maximum possible                          625

  Your running total is floored at 0 after every email — a bad
  start can never dig you into a hole you can't climb out of
  (though a single email's own score can still display as
  negative in Capybara's review, before that floor is applied
  to your total).

DETECTIVE RANK
  Final score is measured as a percentage of the 625-point max.

  Rank   Label                    Score %     Roughly (of 625)
  S      Cyber Detective          95-100%     594-625
  A      Excellent                85-94%      532-593
  B      Good                     70-84%      438-531
  C      Needs Training           50-69%      313-437
  D      Investigation Failed     Below 50%   0-312

SCORE BREAKDOWN ON EVERY REVIEW
  Every Capybara review screen shows the line-by-line breakdown
  above for that specific email — one line per flag placed
  (correct / incorrect) plus the decision line, ending in a
  TOTAL row. Nothing about the math is hidden from the player.


CAPYBARA REVIEW SYSTEM
----------------------------------------------------------------
After every submission, Capybara Detective reviews the
investigation: confirms the verdict (e.g. "PHISHING CONFIRMED"),
walks through each flag placed with a short explanation of why
it was correct, calls out any incorrect flags with a short
explanation of why they weren't real evidence, lists anything
missed with the actual evidence text, and ends with the email's
score and a "Next Email" button.


PHISHING INDICATORS GUIDE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Fake Sender Address
  e.g. security@bpi-secure-login.xyz
  The domain looks related to the real company, but it isn't.

False Urgency
  e.g. "URGENT! Your account will be permanently locked within
  30 minutes unless you verify it."
  Attackers pressure victims into acting fast, without thinking.

Mismatched Link
  Button text:  "Verify Your Account"
  Destination:  https://bpi-account-security.io/login
  The visible text looks safe — the actual destination doesn't.


FINAL RESULTS SCREEN
----------------------------------------------------------------
The Mission Complete screen shows:
  - Emails Investigated (X / 5)
  - Correct Decisions (X / 5)
  - Evidence Found (X / 9)
  - Phishing Detected / Legitimate Identified counts
  - Final Stretch stat (e.g. "2 / 2 On Time") — how many of the
    last two timed emails were submitted before the clock ran
    out
  - Final Score and Detective Rank
